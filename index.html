Course Learning Outcomes

CLO 1Explain data sets graphically, in tabular form, and through sample statistics.
CLO 2Interpret the computed probabilities using popular discrete and continuous probability distributions.
CLO 3Analyze sample statistics to estimate population parameters and construct confidence intervals.
CLO 4Assess simple hypotheses testing to determine statistical significance.
CLO 5Create models for simple linear regression and critically interpret their coefficients.
